Frequently asked questions

Will Books and Music use the same account?

That is the platform design: one Nexauren account, with separate experiences and navigation.

Will Books show sample-pack activity?

No. Books is intended to stay focused on books. Music gets its own storefront and content model.

Where will digital files be stored?

The first version is deliberately not tied to a storage provider yet. Storage will be connected after the commerce and product model are stable.

Are the current books real products?

No live inventory is being claimed in this foundation. The catalogue area is intentionally an empty state until real titles are added.
